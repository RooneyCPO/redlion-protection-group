John Downie leads the Training Department at RedLion Protection Group, with responsibility for developing capable, disciplined, and cohesive close protection teams.

With a distinguished background in military leadership and training, including senior instructional roles within the British Army, John has extensive experience delivering structured, high-performance training programmes. He has led large-scale training operations, managing instructors and recruits through intensive development phases while maintaining the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and operational effectiveness.

His experience extends into close protection training, where he has instructed and mentored delegates across SIA-accredited programmes, focusing on practical skills, teamwork, and professional conduct. Recognised for his leadership and contribution to training excellence — including multiple commendations — he brings a disciplined and results-driven mindset to the development of RedLion's personnel.
